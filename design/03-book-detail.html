Book detail pane

Maps to book_detail_pane.dart. On desktop it is the right-hand column of the two-pane split (5fr / 4fr). On phone it is a full screen pushed onto the navigator. The content and the components are the same; only the container changes.

1. Phone — full screen

Departure from today. Current pane: 110×110 square cover (book covers are not square), a 13px description at alpha: 0.85, chapter rows with a 12px title and a 10px duration, and an all-caps "DESCRIPTION" eyebrow at 10px/α0.4. The download button is the only affordance and it is rust-filled at full width, which reads as the primary action even though most users want stream now. Here streaming is primary and downloading is the secondary call to action. Chapter durations render as "12 min", never "0.0 mins" — the 0-duration bug is treated as a state, shown below.

3. Accessibility contract

  • The pane is a landmark. On desktop the split is role="region" with an accessible name; selecting a tile moves focus into the detail heading so a keyboard user is not stranded.
  • The chapter list is a real list with position announced ("Chapter 3 of 12"). The currently playing chapter uses aria-current.
  • Download progress is a live region announcing at 0/25/50/75/100%, not on every frame.
  • Description text is selectable and reflows to a maximum of 68 characters per line.
