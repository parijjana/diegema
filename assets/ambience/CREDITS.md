source to a seamless loop, levels it well under spoken word, and encodes it as AAC.

Each clip's own page states "License CC0 (public domain)" and names its author (BigSoundBank hosts a
few recordings by other contributors: Clarisse for the cicadas, Pierre Sibanarco for the mountain
stream; they are listed per row above). The nine newer rows were verified on those pages on 2026-10-12;
their source MP3s were downloaded from the same pages and are not stored in the repository.
`battle` (distant period battle) is still missing: no CC0 recording was found.

BigSoundBank releases its sounds under CC0 1.0 Universal (also WTFPL / public domain dedication):
<https://bigsoundbank.com/droit.html>. Attribution isn't required; we credit Joseph Sardin anyway,
in the app (Settings > About, and the Ambience panel) and here. The source MP3s came from the
Aulos project (`Aulos/assets/audio/noise/`), where they were first collected.
