Foundations

Colour ramps, type scale, spacing, radii, elevation and motion for the LibriVox audiobook player. The teal + warm-paper direction is retained and extended into full ramps. Every value below replaces a hardcoded literal in lib/.

1. Colour — primitive ramps

Four hardcoded colours become four ramps. The existing values are preserved as the anchor step of each ramp: #2E6D7D is teal-500, #CC4D22 is rust-500, #F8F8F5 is paper-100, #101417 is ink-900, #181C20 is ink-800, #E6E4DF is bone-200.

Departure from today. The current themes share one accent (#2E6D7D) across light and dark. On the dark surface #181C20 that teal measures 2.94:1 — it fails AA for text and fails even the 3:1 non-text threshold. Dark mode therefore lightens the accent to teal-300 #7FB2BF (7.36:1). The teal identity is unchanged; only the step moves.

3. Type scale

Departure from today. The current widgets use 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14px as raw literals. The floor here is 13px, and 13px is permitted only for information repeated elsewhere. Default reading size is 17px. Book titles in a shelf tile go from 10px to 16px; author lines from 9px to 13px; player timecodes from 10px to 13px tabular.

All-caps is retired. "YOUR LIBRARY IS EMPTY", "IMPORT LOCAL BOOK", "DISCOVER", "NOW PLAYING", "VOLUNTEER FOR LIBRIVOX" and the GlassCard title all use uppercase with letterSpacing: 1.0–2.0. Uppercase costs word-shape recognition, is read letter-by-letter by some screen readers, and is the single strongest "sci-fi HUD" signal in the current UI. Everything becomes sentence case with weight and size carrying the hierarchy. Letter-spacing is 0 everywhere except tabular timecodes.

Departure from today. GlassCard applies a 20-sigma BackdropFilter blur plus an onSurface alpha wash on nearly every surface in the app. It is expensive to composite on every frame, and it makes text contrast depend on whatever scrolls beneath. It is replaced by opaque surfaces with a hairline border and a real shadow scale. Contrast then becomes a property that can be proven rather than estimated.
