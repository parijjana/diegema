Buttons & controls

Five variants, three sizes, and every state. All labels are sentence case — no uppercase, no letter-spacing. Minimum height is 44px in every size, including --sm, because the audience skews older and imprecise pointing is the norm.

1. Variants & sizes

Departure from today. Current buttons are 11px or 10px bold uppercase with letterSpacing: 1.0 and vertical: 6–12 padding — several fall well under 44px tall. Labels also lose their word shape ("IMPORT LOCAL BOOK"). Here every label is 15px sentence case at weight 600, and the box is 44/44/56px tall.

2. States

Disabled state is a real token pair (surface-sunken + text-disabled), not an opacity multiplier — opacity on a translucent surface produces an unpredictable ratio. The focus ring is 3px at --focus-ring with a 2px offset, identical in both themes and on every control.

4. Accessibility contract

  • Every icon-only button carries a Semantics(label:) / tooltip. There are none in lib/ today.
  • Minimum hit box 44×44 logical pixels, enforced by the button itself, not by the caller's padding.
  • Focus ring is drawn by the design system, never suppressed. Keyboard order follows visual order.
  • Disabled controls remain in the accessibility tree with aria-disabled / Semantics(enabled: false) so a screen-reader user learns the control exists and why it is off.
  • Loading buttons announce via aria-busy / a live region, not by silently swapping the label.
