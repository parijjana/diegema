Banners

Three banners: the LibriVox volunteer banner (librivox_volunteer_banner.dart), the auto-resume banner (auto_resume_banner.dart) and the demo-mode notice the canned web build needs. All three share one component with a colour role swap.

1. LibriVox volunteer banner

Departure from today. Current banner reads "VOLUNTEER FOR LIBRIVOX" at 12px/700 with letterSpacing: 1.0, body copy at 11px α0.6, and a "JOIN →" button at 10px uppercase. It also appears twice in the library view — once in the empty state and once above the list. Here it is sentence case at 18px, body at 16px/7.70:1, a 44px-tall button with a real verb, and it appears once per screen. Being the credit to the volunteers who record every book in the app, it is worth reading.

4. Accessibility contract

  • Banners are role="note" / Semantics(container: true) with the title as their accessible name, so a screen reader reads them as one unit.
  • The volunteer banner's link opens externally and says so — "Find out how (opens librivox.org)" in the accessible label.
  • The auto-resume banner is not a live region; announcing it on load would interrupt. It is reachable in normal reading order.
  • The demo notice is announced once on first load via a polite live region.
