Persistent player bar

Maps to persistent_player_bar.dart. Docked to the bottom of every screen while something is loaded. Tapping the identity area opens Now Playing; the transport controls do not open anything, they just work in place.

3. Accessibility contract

  • The bar is a labelled region ("Player"). It is placed after the main content in DOM/semantic order but is reachable by a skip link, so screen-reader users are not forced through it on every screen.
  • The scrub bar is a real slider: aria-valuenow/min/max plus aria-valuetext="12 minutes 41 seconds of 29 minutes 18 seconds". Arrow keys move 15 s, page keys move 60 s.
  • Play/pause is a single toggle whose label changes, never two swapped buttons — the current build swaps Icons.play_circle_filled for Icons.pause_circle_filled with no label at all.
  • Position updates are not a live region; announcing every second would make the app unusable with a screen reader. State changes (play, pause, chapter change, buffering, error) are announced.
  • Media keys and the OS lock-screen controls carry the same title/chapter/artwork metadata.
